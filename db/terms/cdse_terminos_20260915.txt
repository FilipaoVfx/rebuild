1.

Introduction The Copernicus programme (Copernicus) is based on a partnership between the European Union, ESA and the Member States.

ESA coordinates the acquisition and delivery of Earth observation data from space.

Starting with the successful launch of Sentinel-1A in 2014, the series of Sentinel satellites have been delivering a wide range of EO data to diverse user communities worldwide.

The Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem and Data supply falls under the Copernicus Programme evolution, as established with the entry into force of the “Contribution Agreement between the European Commission, representing the European Union, and the European Space Agency on the Implementation of the Union Space Programme and Horizon Europe (Pillar II, Cluster “Digital, Industry and Space”, Space Section)” (in the following referred to as ‘EU-ESA Contribution Agreement') under the “Financial Framework Partnership Agreement between the European Commission, representing the European Union and the European Union Agency for the Space Programme and the European Space Agency on the Implementation of the Union Space Programme and Horizon Europe (Pillar II, Cluster “Digital, Industry and Space”, Space Section)” (FFPA) for the period 2021-2027, and in line with the Regulation (EU)2021/696 of the European Parliament and of the Council of 28 April 2021 establishing the Union Space Programme (the “Space Programme Regulation”).

2.

Data supply The Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem and its online web portal, make Earth observation data available to individuals and entities worldwide.

The web portal allows for the visualisation, searching, processing and download of EO data.

4.

User registration To access Earth observation data at the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem, individuals and entities need to register as a user and provide the requested information.

Through the registration, the user will be granted a password to access EO data.

5.

Personal data privacy When the user accesses the Copernicus Data Space Ecosystem web portal, registers as a user or downloads content, certain information about users, such as internet protocol (IP) addresses, data downloaded, pages visited, software used and time spent, as well as other similar information, will be stored on the servers of Level27 based in Hasselt, Belgium, Kunstlaan 18/4, 3500 and CloudFerro based in Fabryczna 5, 00-511 Warszawa, Poland.

The data centre of Level27 is located in Zaventem, Belgium, the data centre of CloudFerro in Warszawa, Poland.

All data processing facilities and data centers are based in the European Union.

The collected information will be used in accordance with the privacy policy on this web portal.

Whenever sharing user information with individuals or entities other than the parties referred to in the Privacy policy, the data will be dealt with anonymously.

For more information on the Privacy policy please refer to https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/privacy-policy .

6.

Use of cookies Cookies are pieces of information that a web portal transfers to an individual hard drive for record-keeping purposes; passwords are not saved in cookies.

Cookies help to know which pages are visited most often and help to ensure that the web portal responds to users' needs.

If you do not wish to receive a cookie, or if you wish your browser to notify you when you receive a cookie, you may use the option on your web browser to disable cookies.

Click on the 'help' section of your browser to learn how to change cookie preferences.

However, please note that if you disable all cookies, you may not be able to take advantage of all the features available on this web portal.

ESA and T-Systems do not use personal identification details transferred through cookies either for promotion or marketing purposes.

T-Systems will make use of a third-party provider, Piwik Pro, to place cookies on this web portal.

For more information on the Cookie Privacy policy please refer to https://dataspace.copernicus.eu/privacy-policy .
